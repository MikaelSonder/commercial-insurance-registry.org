Jurisdictions

Commercial Insurance jurisdictional reference records

This directory provides access to jurisdictional reference records for Commercial Insurance. Each record describes the commercial service-line in its relevant country, region, state or province, including insurer and intermediary authorisation, insurance distribution, policy and claims frameworks, local-admitted and non-admitted considerations, premium tax, operating constraints and cross-border programme coordination.

Jurisdictional records are editorial references. They are designed to support initial market mapping and corporate risk-management, insurance-placement, broker, insurer, captive, reinsurance or multinational programme planning. They do not replace legal, regulatory, insurance broking, tax, sanctions, data protection, claims or professional advice for a specific placement.
